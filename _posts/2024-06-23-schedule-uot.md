                        <h3 class="timeline-title"><b>Innovation Fair Competition Application Open</b></h3>
                        <p><span class="timeline-desc">Competition</span> | The Innovation Fair marks the first stage of the Innovation OnBoard Pitch Competition. This is your chance to present your groundbreaking business ideas to a panel of judges, entrepreneurs, and esteemed UofT professors. At this dynamic fair, you’ll have the opportunity to showcase your vision, gain valuable feedback, and pitch your concept in a competitive yet supportive environment.</p>
                        <p>Teams will be evaluated on feasibility, innovation, and impact, with the top teams advancing to the next round.</p>
                        <p>To apply, you need a strong business idea, a cohesive and committed team, and at least one team member who is currently, or has been, a UofT student within the past 2 years.</p>
                        <p>Take this opportunity to kickstart your journey in innovation. Submit your application now and join UofT’s vibrant entrepreneurial community.</p>

                        <h3 class="timeline-title">Demystifying fundraising for early stage start-ups</h3>
                        <p><span class="timeline-desc">Location</span> | <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/RgWotjq8hbL3cdW6A" target="_blank">Hart House, Debate Room</a></p>
                       <p><span class="timeline-desc">Speakers|</span> <br>
                       <b>Eva Lau</b> - General Partner, Two Small Fish Ventures <br>
                        <b>Dr. Kamran Khan</b> - Founder & CEO, BlueDot; Professor, Temerty School of Medicine</p>
                        <p><span class="timeline-desc">Fireside Chat</span> | Eva Lau is Co-founder and General Partner at Two Small Fish Ventures, an early-stage VC fund that backs technology startups with the potential to transform user behavior and unlock new value at scale. A recognized leader in Canada’s innovation ecosystem, she mentors through Creative Destruction Lab and UofT’s Hatchery, and contributes to boards and advisory councils including the University of Toronto and the Schulich School of Business. Previously, Eva was Head of Community and Content at Wattpad, where she helped scale the company into a global storytelling platform. She holds an MBA from York University’s Schulich School of Business and a BASc in Industrial Engineering from the University of Toronto. </p>
                        <p>Dr. Kamran Khan is the Founder and CEO of BlueDot, a global health technology company that uses artificial and human intelligence to detect infectious disease threats early and deliver insights that help governments and enterprises strengthen resilience against outbreaks. 
He is also a Professor at the Temerty Faculty of Medicine and the Dalla Lana School of Public Health at the University of Toronto, where he holds a Chair in Health Innovation and Technology. In addition, he serves as a clinician-scientist in infectious diseases at Unity Health Toronto. Previously, he was a medical advisor to the World Health Organization on infectious disease emergencies and global mass gatherings. Dr. Khan earned his MD from the University of Toronto and completed graduate training in public health and infectious diseases at Columbia University and the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health.</p>

Are you looking to join a team and gain experience at a pitch competition? Regardless, join us for the Co-Founder's day, where you can register as either a founder or a co-founder. The event kicks off with an industry-specific speed-dating session, allowing co-founders to meet and explore ideas with founders. Afterward, attendees can network freely during an open social. The event wraps up with team registration, where teams of 1 founder plus 1-3 co-founders are formed for the final pitch competition. </p>
